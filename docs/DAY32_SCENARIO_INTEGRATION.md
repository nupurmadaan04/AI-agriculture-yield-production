# Day 32: Scenario Integration & Comparison Matrix

## 1. Objective & Governing Principles

The Day 32 Scenario Integration subsystem bridges the governed pre-season baseline forecast with the platform's existing what-if simulation architecture (`ScenarioService` & `ScenarioEngine`).

### Core Principles
1. **Simulation $\neq$ Forecast**:
   - The governed point forecast is the baseline generated by `PredictionService` under strict pre-season information boundaries.
   - Scenario outputs are hypothetical what-if simulations conditioned on explicit synthetic or policy perturbations (e.g. fertilizer reallocation, irrigation adjustments, weather shocks).
   - Scenario results are labeled with semantic category `SCENARIO` and never presented as unconditioned forecasts.
2. **Zero Autonomous Ranking**:
   - The comparison matrix strictly forbids subjective evaluation terms: `BEST`, `WORST`, `WINNER`, `RECOMMENDED`, `OPTIMAL`.
   - All comparisons are displayed transparently and quantitatively: Projected Yield ($\text{kg/ha}$), Absolute Delta ($\text{kg/ha}$), Relative Delta (%), Assumptions, Uncertainty, and Limitations.
3. **No Fabricated Capabilities**:
   - Only scenarios supported by `ScenarioService` parameter archetypes and perturbation bounds are generated.
   - If an input perturbation exceeds governed physical limits or lacks empirical model backing, it reports structured limitations.

## 3. Supported Scenario Archetypes & Parameters

The workspace exposes standard archetypes configured within governed operational bounds:

| Scenario ID | Name | Perturbation Description | Evidence Category | Limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `baseline` | Governed Baseline | Certified pre-season forecast under baseline conditions | `PREDICTED` | Pre-season information only; subject to in-season weather variability |
| `conservative` | Conservative Improvement | Modest +5% fertilizer efficiency, +3% irrigation optimization | `SCENARIO` | Linear sensitivity approximation; assumes optimal application timing |
| `moderate` | Moderate Growth | Active management with +10% nutrient management, +7% water access | `SCENARIO` | Assumes no severe adverse climate shocks or pest outbreaks |
| `stress` | Adverse Weather & Stress | -15% rainfall anomaly, -10% input contraction | `SCENARIO` | Downside sensitivity model; non-linear compound drought impacts may exceed model scope |
| `custom` | Custom User Scenario | User-defined perturbations within $[-30\%, +30\%]$ bounds | `SCENARIO` | User-driven hypothesis testing; not an authoritative prediction |

---

## 4. Uncertainty in Scenarios

- Where underlying ML ensembles support uncertainty propagation, P10–P90 spread is reported.
- Where scenario-specific uncertainty cannot be rigorously derived (e.g. deterministic baseline strategies or uncalibrated perturbation spaces), the system returns:
  > *"Uncertainty not available for this scenario."*
- Uncertainty bounds are never artificially generated, hallucinated, or interpolated.

---

## 5. Non-Autonomous Decision Support Statement

> **Governing Mandate**: The Decision Workspace is an evidence-first analytical workbench designed to inform human decision-makers. The system provides objective deltas and explicit limitations. The selection of an action, risk tolerance, and policy direction resides exclusively with the human decision-maker.
