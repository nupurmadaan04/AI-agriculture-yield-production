# DAY 36 — Master Claim Register

This register inventories all major quantitative, scientific, algorithmic, and engineering claims across the Agricultural Forecasting & Decision Intelligence platform, documenting their source files, metrics, populations, validation protocols, reproducibility statuses, and evidentiary foundations.

---

## Claim Status Taxonomy

- **`REPRODUCED`**: Independently regenerated or verified directly against underlying raw data, scripts, and model artifacts within defined numerical tolerances.
- **`SUPPORTED`**: Underlying code, metadata, or model artifacts support the claim, but full retraining from raw scratch was not independently executed.
- **`DOCUMENTED_ONLY`**: The claim appears in documentation or legacy reports but cannot be independently verified from available artifacts.
- **`INCONSISTENT`**: Different authoritative sources disagree or cite conflicting metrics for the same experiment.
- **`UNSUPPORTED`**: Evidence directly contradicts the claim.
- **`NOT_APPLICABLE`**: Architectural or qualitative claim that does not involve numerical reproduction.
