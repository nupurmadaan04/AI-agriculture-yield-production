## 2. Medium Description (100–150 words)
The AI Agriculture Decision Intelligence Platform transforms historical agricultural data into actionable, auditable decision briefs for agronomists and policymakers. Operating on a verified ICRISAT panel of 311 Indian districts (1966–2017), the platform integrates pre-season and post-harvest Random Forest forecasting, Isolation Forest anomaly detection, spatial clustering, and CUSUM early-warning monitoring. 

Evaluated under strict chronological out-of-time validation ($2013-2017$), the forecasting engine achieves an $R^2$ of 0.7866 and MAE of 353.01 kg/ha. Predictions are paired with Tree SHAP feature attributions, constrained SLSQP scenario optimization, and multi-domain evidence fusion. Every decision brief features an end-to-end Statement $\rightarrow$ Model $\rightarrow$ Dataset provenance DAG and a deterministic SHA-256 cryptographic audit certificate, providing transparent, scientifically grounded decision support.

## 3. Detailed Description (250–300 words)
The AI Agriculture Decision Intelligence Platform is an enterprise-grade decision-support system designed to address the challenges of agricultural risk assessment, regional productivity planning, and food security governance. Traditional agricultural ML models often suffer from optimistic bias due to random data splitting, lack explainability, and fail to provide auditable justification for recommendations.

This platform bridges empirical data science and policy intelligence by unifying eight core analytical layers:
1. **Data Engineering**: Processed a 51-year ICRISAT district panel (1966–2017) spanning 2,469 clean observations across 311 districts in 20 Indian states, incorporating rolling lagged yield features and cyclical climate transformations.
2. **Machine Learning & Reliability**: Deployed Random Forest pipelines evaluated via chronological out-of-time validation ($2013-2017$), achieving an $R^2$ of 0.7866, MAE of 353.01 kg/ha, RMSE of 513.11 kg/ha, and MAPE of 18.04%, with 10-decile prediction spread calibration (81.3% empirical coverage) and PSI drift monitoring.
3. **Geospatial & Temporal Monitoring**: Implemented spatial K-Means clustering, Local Moran's I spatial autocorrelation, multi-window moving averages, and two-sided CUSUM change-point tracking across 311 districts.
4. **Explainability & Scenarios**: Developed local Tree SHAP feature attribution curves and constrained SLSQP scenario optimization with physical non-negativity bounds.
5. **Decision Intelligence & Cryptographic Auditability**: Formulated multi-module evidence fusion ranking analytical priorities (`HIGH`, `MODERATE`, `LOW`), mapped statements to raw data via a Provenance DAG, and generated deterministic SHA-256 decision audit certificates (`DEC-xxxxxxxx`).
6. **Full-Stack Production**: Engineered a high-performance FastAPI backend with request tracing and deep health/readiness probes, a responsive React 18 / TypeScript SPA, and single-command Docker deployment.
